FOUNDER’S VISION

Technology is not measured by how much it can do, but by whether it knows when to stop.

Helena Vale is the fictional founder and chair of ELIAS NET. She builds the company on one principle: the further autonomy enters the physical world, the clearer human authorization, stop authority and accountability must become.

Teach AI its boundaries before it enters the workflow. Keep human accountability before expanding automation.

GOVERNANCE PROTOCOL / FROM INTENT TO MONITORING

Every step forward passes through a gate people can understand.

Governance is not a disclaimer at the bottom of a page. It is how the company makes decisions every day.

01

FRAME THE WORK

Define the work to improve, who is affected and what must not change.

02

CHECK THE DATA

Check where information comes from, who can see it, whether it is sufficient and what must be excluded.

03

SET THE BOUNDARY

Set what AI may read, suggest, organize or do; pause before high-impact actions.

04

TEST THE RESULT

Challenge assumptions with real work situations, checking answers, flows and exceptions.

05

AUTHORIZE THE ACTION

An accountable person decides whether the work can move forward and records why.

06

MONITOR THE CHANGE

Track changes, incidents and use so the team knows when to adjust, pause or roll back.

THREE PRODUCT SYSTEMS / COMPANY MATRIX

Three products. One human-command layer.

NETOPS is the near-term lab core. GUARDIAN and MEDIC are concept products. Each one makes inputs, AI assistance, human control and verifiable outcomes explicit.

LAB
01ELIAS NET

ELIAS NETOPS

For IT, operations and security teams

Bring monitoring, logs, security and backup events into one view so teams understand what happened before deciding what to do.

SAFETY BOUNDARYControlled recommendations only; no direct changes to production.
HOW IT WORKSINPUT → ASSIST → CONTROL → EVIDENCE
  1. 01INPUT

    Monitoring, log, security and backup events

  2. 02AI ASSISTANCE

    Correlate events, summarize impact and suggest a check path

  3. 03HUMAN CONTROL

    IT approves, rejects or stops the response

  4. 04VERIFIABLE RESULT

    Incident context, action record and traceable trace

02ELIAS NET

ELIAS GUARDIAN

For rescue, inspection and critical mission teams

Coordinate unarmed robots, unmanned vehicles, sensors and communications resources so field teams can understand the situation before planning the next step.

SAFETY BOUNDARYNo weapon control, target selection or autonomous high-risk action.
HOW IT WORKSINPUT → ASSIST → CONTROL → EVIDENCE
  1. 01INPUT

    Location, sensors, available resources and communications status

  2. 02AI ASSISTANCE

    Organize the field picture and suggest routes and resource options

  3. 03HUMAN CONTROL

    A rescue lead authorizes, reassigns or stops the mission

  4. 04VERIFIABLE RESULT

    Mission status, handoff record and resource trail

CONCEPT
03ELIAS NET

ELIAS MEDIC

For medical, care and rescue coordination teams

Bring location, vital signs, care information and rescue resources together so qualified professionals can hand off and decide sooner.

SAFETY BOUNDARYNo diagnosis, replacement of clinicians or autonomous medical conclusion.
HOW IT WORKSINPUT → ASSIST → CONTROL → EVIDENCE
  1. 01INPUT

    Location, vital signs, care records and rescue resources

  2. 02AI ASSISTANCE

    Summarize the situation, flag gaps and prepare a handoff brief

  3. 03HUMAN CONTROL

    Qualified medical or response staff decide, approve or take over

  4. 04VERIFIABLE RESULT

    Sourced handoff brief, timeline and accountability record
