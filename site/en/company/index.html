GOVERNANCE PROTOCOL / FROM INTENT TO MONITORING

Every step forward passes through a gate people can understand.

Governance is not a disclaimer at the bottom of a page. It is how the company makes decisions every day.

01

FRAME THE WORK

Define the work to improve, who is affected and what must not change.

02

CHECK THE DATA

Check where information comes from, who can see it, whether it is sufficient and what must be excluded.

03

SET THE BOUNDARY

Set what AI may read, suggest, organize or do; pause before high-impact actions.

04

TEST THE RESULT

Challenge assumptions with real work situations, checking answers, flows and exceptions.

05

AUTHORIZE THE ACTION

An accountable person decides whether the work can move forward and records why.

06

MONITOR THE CHANGE

Track changes, incidents and use so the team knows when to adjust, pause or roll back.
